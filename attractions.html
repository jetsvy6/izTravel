Attractions and views

Eleven places worth a long drive.

Maiden Tower and Baku Boulevard

Baku, Azerbaijan

A 12th-century stone tower stands inside the Old City, a short walk from the Caspian seafront promenade.

Did you know? The tower's walls are up to five metres thick.

Find a bus tour

Shahdag Mountains

Qusar, Azerbaijan

A high Caucasus range with snow from winter to spring and green meadows in summer.

Did you know? Peaks here exceed 4,000 metres.

Find a bus tour

Nohur Lake

Gabala, Azerbaijan

A clear, forest-ringed lake that is ideal for walking and boat rides.

Did you know? It is fed by mountain springs.

Find a bus tour

Seven Beauties Waterfall

Gabala, Azerbaijan

A series of cascades dropping through beech forest, reached by a short trail.

Did you know? The name refers to its seven tiers.

Find a bus tour

Sheki Khan's Palace

Sheki, Azerbaijan

A summer palace from the 18th century, known for its shebeke windows of coloured glass.

Did you know? The windows are assembled without glue or nails.

Find a bus tour

Khinalig Village

Guba, Azerbaijan

A stone village at around 2,300 metres where residents speak a unique language.

Did you know? Khinalig is among the highest settlements in Europe.

Find a bus tour

Gobustan

Near Baku, Azerbaijan

A UNESCO site of rock engravings, mud volcanoes and open desert views.

Did you know? Some engravings are thousands of years old.

Find a bus tour

Kazbegi and Gergeti Trinity Church

Kazbegi, Georgia

A 14th-century church stands alone on a ridge below Mount Kazbek.

Did you know? It sits at roughly 2,170 metres above sea level.

Find a bus tour

Batumi Boulevard

Batumi, Georgia

A long seaside promenade with parks, fountains and a modern skyline.

Did you know? It runs for several kilometres along the Black Sea.

Find a bus tour

Uzungol Lake

Trabzon, Turkey

An alpine lake with pine slopes and wooden houses at its edge.

Did you know? The name means Long Lake in Turkish.

Find a bus tour

Cappadocia

Central Anatolia, Turkey

Soft volcanic rock carved into houses, churches and underground cities.

Did you know? Hot-air balloons fly here but our tours focus on the valleys by bus.

Find a bus tour
